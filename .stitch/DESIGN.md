# Design System: Jovina Diniz — Terapeuta Integrativa & Mentora

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
A warm, sophisticated and emotionally grounded identity for women seeking reconnection, self-understanding and direction. The visual language should feel mature, intimate and premium without becoming clinical, mystical, overly feminine or generic coaching. Think editorial wellness with quiet confidence: generous negative space, asymmetric composition, tactile natural materials and subtle human warmth.

- Density: 3/10 — airy, calm, intentional.
- Variance: 6/10 — asymmetric but composed.
- Motion: 4/10 — restrained, fluid, never distracting.
- Core idea: **“Voltar para si.”**
- Brand values: acolhimento, identidade, consciência, profundidade, direção, maturidade.

## 2. Color Palette & Roles
- **Warm Ivory** (#F6F1E8) — primary canvas and page background.
- **Soft Porcelain** (#FBF8F2) — elevated surfaces and quiet content blocks.
- **Deep Umber** (#2E2A25) — primary text and high-contrast brand ink.
- **Muted Taupe** (#756D63) — secondary text, captions and supporting copy.
- **Linen Border** (#DDD3C4) — hairline dividers and subtle structure.
- **Olive Identity** (#66704A) — the single interactive accent for primary CTA, focus and active states.
- **Caramel Earth** (#B07B5A) — decorative neutral for framing, editorial details and brand imagery; never used as a competing CTA accent.

Never use pure black, neon colors, saturated purple/blue gradients or high-gloss metallic effects.

## 3. Typography Rules
- **Display / Brand Headlines:** Bodoni Moda — elegant editorial contrast, controlled tracking, used selectively.
- **Body / UI:** DM Sans — calm, contemporary, highly legible.
- **Labels / Metadata:** DM Sans Medium — uppercase only when short and intentional.
- Headlines should feel authored, not oversized. Use hierarchy through weight, rhythm and whitespace.
- Body copy: relaxed leading, maximum 62–65 characters per line.
- Avoid Inter and generic system-font styling.

## 4. Logo System
### Primary Wordmark
“Jovina Diniz” as an elegant editorial wordmark, with refined spacing and a small supporting descriptor: “Terapeuta Integrativa & Mentora”.

### Secondary Monogram
A custom **JD** monogram built from two continuous, restrained curves suggesting an inward return and a forward path. It should feel like a personal seal, not a decorative icon.

### Symbol Philosophy
The mark should communicate **reconnection with self + identity + direction** through negative space and movement. Avoid obvious therapy clichés.

### Banned Logo Motifs
No lotus, butterfly, brain, heart outline, hands, female silhouette, tree-of-life, moon phases, mandala, generic leaf, infinity symbol or medical cross.

## 5. Component Stylings
- **Buttons:** Soft rectangular shape with 12–14px radius, Olive Identity fill for primary actions, Deep Umber text when outlined. Tactile 1px press movement; no glow.
- **Cards:** Use sparingly. Prefer editorial sections divided by whitespace and Linen Border lines. When needed, 20–24px radius with almost imperceptible warm shadow.
- **Inputs:** Label above field, warm off-white surface, 1px Linen Border, Olive focus ring, clear error text below.
- **Testimonials:** Editorial quote treatment rather than equal-card grids.
- **Image Frames:** Organic editorial crops with restrained rounded corners; no blob shapes.

## 6. Layout Principles
- Maximum content width around 1280–1360px.
- Asymmetric hero and editorial split layouts.
- Avoid centered generic landing-page composition.
- No three equal feature cards in a row.
- Use 12-column grid on desktop and single-column collapse below 768px.
- Mobile touch targets minimum 44px.
- Generous section rhythm using clamp-based vertical spacing.
- No overlapping text and images.

## 7. Imagery Direction
Photography should feel real, calm and intimate: natural window light, warm neutral environments, tactile materials, quiet gestures, women in reflective but empowered moments. Avoid staged corporate stock-photo smiles, spa clichés and exaggerated “healing” symbolism.

For the professional portrait, reserve a flexible portrait frame so the final photo can be replaced later without redesigning the composition.

## 8. Motion & Interaction
Use subtle opacity/transform reveals, soft staggered entrances and restrained hover movement. No perpetual attention-seeking animation. Motion should reinforce calm and clarity.

## 9. Voice & Copy Tone
Warm, mature, direct and reflective. Avoid promises of cure, urgency marketing and generic self-help clichés. Prefer language around understanding, reconstruction, consciousness, identity and direction.

## 10. Anti-Patterns (Banned)
- No emojis in the interface.
- No neon glow, purple/blue tech gradients or pure black.
- No generic “coach” aesthetic.
- No mystical wellness clichés.
- No generic 3-column card rows.
- No fake statistics or fabricated credentials.
- No exaggerated medical/therapeutic claims.
- No cliché logo symbols listed above.
- No centered hero when an asymmetric editorial composition is possible.
