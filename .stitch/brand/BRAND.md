# Brand Foundation — Jovina Diniz

## Brand Name
**Jovina Diniz**

Supporting descriptor: **Terapeuta Integrativa & Mentora de Mulheres**

Social handle: **@terapeutadiniz**

## Strategic Brand Idea
### “Voltar para si.”
The brand represents the movement of returning to one’s own story, understanding patterns, rebuilding identity and choosing a more conscious direction.

## Positioning
Jovina Diniz supports women through individual therapy, mentoring, group work and corporate talks, with a focus on identity, emotional awareness and personal direction.

## Core Promise
Create a safe, thoughtful space in which women can understand their history, rebuild their identity and make more coherent choices about the life they want to live.

## Pillars
1. **Identidade** — rediscovering who one is beyond roles, wounds and external expectations.
2. **Consciência** — understanding one’s history, patterns and emotional dynamics.
3. **Direção** — translating insight into choices and a more coherent life.
4. **Acolhimento** — depth without judgment or theatricality.

## Personality
- Warm, not sugary.
- Sophisticated, not distant.
- Feminine, not ornamental.
- Deep, not heavy.
- Contemporary, not trendy.
- Confident, not authoritarian.

## Logo Direction
### Route A — Editorial Wordmark
A refined “Jovina Diniz” wordmark with distinctive typographic rhythm. Best for the website header, documents and professional presence.

### Route B — JD Personal Seal
A custom JD monogram formed by two continuous curves. The inner movement represents returning inward; the open terminal represents direction and continuation.

### Route C — Wordmark + Signature Line
Primary wordmark paired with “Terapeuta Integrativa & Mentora” in restrained sans-serif typography for institutional use.

## Logo Construction Rules
- Avoid literal symbols.
- Favor negative space.
- Keep line weight elegant but strong enough for small digital use.
- The monogram must work at favicon / social avatar size.
- Provide one-color versions in Deep Umber, Olive Identity and reversed Warm Ivory.

## Visual Keywords
Editorial, grounded, warm, feminine maturity, quiet confidence, natural materiality, introspection, identity, direction.

## Suggested Brand Phrase
**“Compreender sua história. Reconstruir sua identidade. Escolher sua direção.”**

## Website Hero Candidate
**“Um espaço para compreender sua história, reconstruir sua identidade e viver com mais verdade.”**
